📘 Paper 1: A Blockchain-Based Secure Data Transmission Architecture (IEEE)
📌 What this paper proposes

This paper proposes a blockchain-based architecture to secure data transmission over computer networks. It integrates cryptographic encryption and hashing with blockchain technology to prevent data tampering, remove single points of failure, and improve trust in network communication.

✅ Good for:

✔ Network data transmission
✔ Network security
✔ Blockchain + cryptography integration

📌 Why it is useful for your project

Directly focuses on secure data transmission

Clearly shows why encryption alone is insufficient

Uses blockchain as a tamper-proof audit mechanism

Very suitable for CIP-level understanding

📚 Use in your work

Core literature review paper

Base paper for problem statement

Reference for architecture inspiration
